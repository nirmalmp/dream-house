Open terrace

The central terrace is approximately 354 sq ft after the Tulsi void and roof stair landing. It is intentionally kept mostly clear for kite flying.

  • Direct stair access uses the same 6′ × 10′ L-shaped footprint as the floors below.
  • Terracotta roof zones cover the enclosed bedrooms and center suite; the terrace remains open to the sky.
  • 3′ 6″ parapet and non-slip exterior tile are indicated for the terrace concept.
  • Small seating stays at the edge, clear of the kite-flying area.
