Two-bedroom first floor

Stair access

The L-shaped stairwell at the front-left of the central hall is stacked directly above the ground-floor stairs. It serves both down to the ground floor and up to the roof. Both levels share the same 6′ × 10′ bounding footprint and turn position. The separate staircase in the center gallery has been removed.

Front bedrooms and gallery

The double-height opening is removed. Both side bedrooms sit toward the front, with their bathrooms and closets immediately behind them. The private galleries are removed; the left rear terrace and adjoining gallery remain connected without a partition. Only the 8′ × 6′ gallery is covered; the 14′ × 8′ terrace stays open to the sky. The former right rear closet is removed and is now a 14′ × 14′ open roof terrace. The relocated bathrooms no longer sit directly above the ground-floor bathrooms, so plumbing routes need updating. Covered side balconies extend 6′ forward and connect across a 19′ × 6′ gallery above the ground-floor porch. The front galleries have roofs and open sides.

Each side of the porch shows one car and one bike below the gallery. The separate open bike spaces are removed. Total: two cars and two bikes. The separate side-yard carports are removed. Parking symbols are illustrative; actual vehicle, door and maneuvering clearances within the 14′ × 6′ covers need checking.

Central spaces

The 14′ × 8′ open rear terrace connects to the 8′ × 6′ covered gallery without a partition. A dashed line marks the roof edge, not a wall. The gallery opens to the center master bedroom. The center bathroom is entered from the bedroom through an inward-opening door near the top of its right wall. The bathroom basin is at the bottom, and the bed’s headboard is against the top wall with a 6′ workspace to its right. The rear-right closet’s left wall aligns with the bathroom below. Its size is 8′ × 8′, and the freed 6′ × 8′ space joins the center bedroom and contains its workspace. The closet is accessible from that bedroom; the standalone bedroom wardrobe is removed.

The porch remains an entrance below the new center gallery. The gallery provides the cover for parking below.
